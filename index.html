DEPED CURRICULUMS

The Department of Education (DepEd) is implementing the MATATAG Curriculum, a revised K-10 basic education program designed to decongest the previous K-12 setup by 70% and focus heavily on foundational skills.

Junior High School

The curriculum is divided into Core Subjects taken by all students, and Special Curricular Programs (SCPs) for students with specific talents or interests.

Senior High School

The curriculum consists of 15 Core Subjects (mandatory for everyone) and specialized subjects depending on the chosen Track and Strand.

Special Needs / Inclusive Education (SNED)

This utilizes a Modified K to 12 Curriculum and a Transition Curriculum tailored to the unique learning pace and capabilities of Learners with Disabilities (LWDs).

Special Program

help students develop their unique talents, interests, and multiple intelligences beyond the core academic curriculum.
